# Solo Master

A personal mastery coach: one learner builds durable, unaided skill through retrieval, practice, feedback, and delayed fresh checks.

## Learning

**Learning Path**:
An ordered set of Artifacts that serves one Mission.
_Avoid_: Track, course, curriculum (for a single path)

**Mission**:
The learner-written goal of a Learning Path: what success looks like, the success criteria, and what is out of scope.
_Avoid_: Objective, goal (as a product term)

**Artifact**:
One small, reviewed unit of what is learned, with its content, mastery requirements, and exercises.
_Avoid_: Lesson, topic, card

**Exercise**:
A way to elicit evidence for an Artifact, tagged with one Activity Type.
_Avoid_: Question, task

**Activity Type**:
The kind of learning activity an Exercise is, such as a retrieval prompt, a worked example with self-explanation, or a faded completion.

**Attempt**:
What the learner actually did on one Exercise, including how much assistance they had.
_Avoid_: Answer, submission

**Evidence**:
The judged result of an Attempt for one mastery dimension, marked as aided or unaided and fresh or repeated. It may carry misconception tags.
_Avoid_: Score, grade

**Aided**:
Work done with any help visible or available, such as a hint, a worked example, a Reference Sheet, or AI feedback. Aided Evidence never counts toward Mastered.

**Worked Example**:
A fully or partly solved problem shown to the learner. Studying one is never Evidence. Explaining one while it is visible is Aided.

**Reference Sheet**:
Reviewed, compressed notes for an Artifact or Learning Path. Always Aided material, and never available during a Mastery Check.
_Avoid_: Cheat sheet, notes

**Draft**:
A proposed Artifact or Reference Sheet, usually derived from a research brief, that Learning holds until the learner reviews it. Only an approved Draft enters a Learning Path.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, pending item

**Placement**:
The learner's starting point on a Learning Path, from disclosed prior knowledge or a diagnostic probe. It can bring a Mastery Check forward but is never mastery Evidence.
_Avoid_: Pre-test, level

**Calibration**:
How well the learner's stated confidence matches the outcome of their Attempts. High-confidence errors are flagged first.

**Mastery Check**:
A fresh, unaided Exercise whose Evidence can move an Artifact toward Mastered.

**Mastered**:
The state an Artifact reaches after one immediate and two delayed Mastery Checks pass at growing gaps, judged by application-owned rules. It is not permanent: spaced reviews continue, and a failed review returns the Artifact to Learning for targeted relearning.
_Avoid_: Learned, done, complete

**Review Due**:
The state of a Mastered Artifact whose next spaced review has arrived.

**Learning Method**:
A policy that chooses which Activity Types are offered, their order, their timing, and which count as Aided. It never changes how Evidence qualifies or when an Artifact becomes Mastered. There is one built-in method until a second sequence is really needed.
_Avoid_: Mode, strategy, technique
